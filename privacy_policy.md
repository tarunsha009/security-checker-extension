### Privacy Policy for Security Checker Chrome Extension

---

**Privacy Policy**

**Last Updated**: [18-10-2024]

**Security Checker** ("the Extension") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle any data when you use the extension.

### 1. **Information We Collect**
The **Security Checker** extension does **not** collect, store, or share any personal information. The extension only checks the SSL certificate status of websites visited, but it does not track, log, or transmit any user activity or personal data.

### 2. **Third-Party Services**
The extension does not interact with third-party services or track any of your browsing activity outside of retrieving SSL information about websites.

### 3. **Data Usage**
No data is collected or used by this extension for any purpose other than displaying the SSL certificate status of the websites you visit.

### 4. **Security**
We prioritize user privacy and security. The **Security Checker** extension does not collect or handle any sensitive information, ensuring that your personal data remains private and secure at all times.

### 5. **Changes to this Policy**
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with the updated date.

### 6. **Contact Us**
If you have any questions or concerns regarding this Privacy Policy, feel free to contact us via our GitHub repository: [https://github.com/tarunsha009/security-checker-extension](https://github.com/tarunsha009/security-checker-extension).
